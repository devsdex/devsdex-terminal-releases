Features

Less switching.
More doing.

SSH

A terminal for every server.

Keep independent SSH sessions in tabs. Search your output, resize your terminal, and use your familiar command-line tools.

bash / zsh / vim / tmux / htop

SFTP

Files, right beside your shell.

Browse remote folders and drag files between your Mac and your server. Follow progress in one transfer queue.

KEYCHAIN

Less typing. Your Keychain.

Organize connections into groups and reuse private keys. Choose which passwords and passphrases to remember in macOS Keychain.

PRIVACY

Your connections stay on your Mac.

No account, analytics, or terminal recording. Connections are stored locally. Passwords and private keys are stored in macOS Keychain when you choose to save them. The app connects directly to the servers you select.

Optional update checks contact our public release host. This website and downloads are hosted on GitHub, whose infrastructure may keep standard access logs. Diagnostic files are shared only when you export and send them yourself.
